# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-23)

First release of **SpellRange**: action bar keybinds that turn red when your target is out of range.

### Features
- **Red keybinds when out of range:** the keybind number on an action button turns red when your target is out of range, and white again when it's in range.
- **Optional red icon tint:** tint the whole spell icon red when out of range. Icons go back to their normal colors (including grey for unusable and blue for out of mana) when you're in range again.
- **Bold, readable keybinds:** a thick outline and a drop shadow on keybind numbers, with an adjustable font size (8–22).
- **Optional background box:** a dark box behind keybind numbers for extra contrast on bright icons, with adjustable darkness.
- **Instant updates** when you change target and when you enter or leave combat.
- An options page in **Esc → Options → AddOns → SpellRange** (or `/sr`), and `/sr help` for all commands.
